# Brand system

Read with `AI_SETUP.md`, `COMPONENT_RULES.md`, and `VISUAL_QA.md` before changing public UI. This system describes the existing portfolio; it is not permission to invent work or change disclosure boundaries.

## Personality and recognition

Yahya's portfolio is calm, direct, technically literate, and accountable. It is recognizable through the combination of a real portrait, actual product and systems work, explicit contribution and outcome boundaries, and clear paths for a recruiter to check the evidence. Put authored details and project artifacts ahead of visual effects. Keep the writing plain and specific.

## Foundations

- **Colour:** off-white `#f7f9fb`, white `#fff`, slate and ink `#334155` / `#0f172a`, subtle dividers `#e2e8f0`. Teal `#38b2ac` with dark text-safe teal `#226b68` is the one accent. Neutral status colours may communicate errors or warnings, never act as a second brand accent.
- **One-accent rule:** one teal action or point of emphasis per visual group. Do not colour every label, heading fragment, border, icon, and link at once. Primary buttons can stay ink; teal is especially useful for links, focus, and the active state.
- **Type:** local Plus Jakarta Sans is the reading and display face. Hero and page titles are the largest tier, section headings the middle tier, body at 16px or more, and metadata at a readable 12–13px minimum. Use weight and spacing before colour. Avoid italics for arbitrary emphasis. Space Mono is justified only for real code, terminal output, identifiers, or short machine-like values that benefit from fixed alignment. It is not the default for navigation, tags, dates, captions, filters, or prose.
- **Spacing and density:** use a consistent page gutter and generous space between major sections. Keep related content close enough to scan. Avoid empty boxes, huge card padding, and tiny text used to force content into a fixed height. Text paragraphs should usually fit about 55–75 characters per line.
- **Images:** use approved, existing portfolio artwork and actual project or portrait images. Preserve descriptive alt text, aspect ratio, responsive sources, and explicit dimensions. Give diagrams captions that identify what they show and their source or scope. Never imply an illustrative concept is a shipped screen.
- **Borders and radii:** dividers organize lists and evidence; thin borders can contain interactive images, forms, and controls. Use compact 2–8px radii. A section does not need a container merely to feel designed.
- **Motion:** short feedback only. Never use floating ornamental motion, perpetual decoration, or effects that interrupt reading. Respect reduced motion.
- **Hierarchy:** identity → work → evidence → next action. Keep one clear action per section and quieter secondary links. Real work can anchor a composition more convincingly than a generic tech graphic.
- **Tone:** first-person where useful, specific about role and delivery, and honest about unknown outcomes. Avoid generic collaboration slogans, inflated impact, and unexplained jargon.

## Rejection criteria

Do not introduce purple-blue gradients, glows, HUD grids, fake dashboards, floating labels, decorative diagrams, arbitrary symbols as icons, motivational CTA bands, unsupported charts or numbers, stock imagery, highlighter pills, generic loop arrows, coloured side stripes, or endlessly repeated identical cards. Do not add unapproved brand assets. Do not disclose locked VR details or StarLink/customer material. A visual treatment earns its place by improving understanding, navigation, or evidence.
